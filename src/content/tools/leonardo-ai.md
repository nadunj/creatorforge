Leonardo AI is built around the problem most single-image generators ignore:
producing dozens of images that all look like they belong to the same video.
Its fine-tuned models and style presets make that far more consistent than
prompting a general-purpose generator from scratch each time.

## How it works

You pick a model (realistic, illustrative, anime, etc.), write a prompt, and
generate in batches. Custom/fine-tuned models let you lock a specific visual
profile — useful for explainer or historical-reconstruction channels that
need every image to look like the same "world."

## Pricing, in practice

The token system is the main source of confusion: higher resolutions and
certain models cost more tokens per generation, so a plan that sounds
generous in image *count* can run out fast at higher settings — compare it
against [Midjourney](/tools/midjourney/) and [ChatGPT Images](/tools/chatgpt-images/)
before committing to a plan.

## Who it's for

Faceless YouTube channels and explainer creators generating many images per
video who need style consistency more than single-image peak quality.
