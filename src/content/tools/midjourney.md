Midjourney remains the reference point for AI image quality — when a
generated image looks unmistakably "AI art" in a good way, it's often
Midjourney's aesthetic people are describing.

## How it works

Prompt in, four image variations out, with upscale and remix options. The
web app has closed some of the gap with Discord's power-user prompting
tools, but Discord is still where most detailed prompt engineering happens.

## Who it's for

Thumbnail generation and single hero images where visual punch drives
click-through rate. For dozens of consistent in-video images across a whole
script, [Leonardo AI](/tools/leonardo-ai/) or [ChatGPT Images](/tools/chatgpt-images/)
are usually a better fit — see the full [image generator comparison](/compare/leonardo-vs-midjourney-vs-chatgpt-images/).
