VidIQ layers keyword research and scoring directly into the YouTube upload
flow, which matters more than it sounds — creators skip standalone SEO tools
far more often than ones built into their existing workflow.

## How it works

Install the browser extension, and it overlays keyword scores, competitor
data, and trend alerts directly on YouTube Studio and video pages. The
standalone dashboard adds deeper keyword research and tracking.

## Who it's for

Any channel treating YouTube as a search engine rather than only a
recommendation feed — useful alongside a title/thumbnail strategy for
[faceless YouTube channels](/guides/best-ai-tools-for-faceless-youtube-channels/)
specifically, where you don't have a personal brand doing discovery work for you.
