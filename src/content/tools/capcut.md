CapCut is the default answer to "what should I edit Shorts in" for a reason —
it's free, mobile-native, and built specifically around the vertical,
caption-heavy format that YouTube Shorts, Reels, and TikTok all reward.

## How it works

Import clips, drop them on a timeline, add auto-captions, apply a transition
or text template, export in the correct aspect ratio for your platform. The
mobile app is the primary product; desktop exists but trails it in maturity.

## Who it's for

Anyone producing Shorts or Reels who wants a fast, free, mobile-first editing
workflow rather than a full desktop NLE. Pair it with an AI voice tool like
[ElevenLabs](/tools/elevenlabs/) or [Gemini TTS](/tools/gemini-tts/) for
narrated Shorts.
